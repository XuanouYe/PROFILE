At Bayerische Staatsoper, Damiano Michieletto’s 2023 production. The stage is highly abstract, with an off-centre set moving up and down, as though asking whether to descend into hell or ascend to heaven. Everything else is a symbolic narrative of violence: an indoor sports hall and heaps of ash, invoking historical wars to address the present.
