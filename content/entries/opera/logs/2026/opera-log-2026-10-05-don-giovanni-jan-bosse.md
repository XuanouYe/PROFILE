A directorial approach in which the visual takes precedence over everything else. Silent figure of "Death" appears onstage. Turning an abstract concept into a bodily performance is something I feel like I have seen many times, but the idea is particularly prominent here. During the overture, Death climbs out of the orchestra pit onto the stage, an androgynous, pale, skeletal ghost inhabiting both curtain and stage. At times it watches the characters in an almost animal posture, at others it becomes Don Giovanni’s mirror image. The revolving set has a dilapidated exterior on one side and a cheap, glittering nightclub interior on the other. Everything spills out from this vulgar, desire-driven interior, tracing a closed cycle of fate.
