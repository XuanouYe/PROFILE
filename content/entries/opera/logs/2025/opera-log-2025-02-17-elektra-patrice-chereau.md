Patrice Chéreau's production in his late years. A simple, constricted outdoor setting tells the complex ancient Greek revenge myth. The soprano’s extreme singing, continually pushed towards a climax, and the orchestra’s highly dynamic playing interact with the fear of death. Every pause, silence, stare, approach and retreat conceals a crisis about to erupt. Under this kind of writing, the conflict between reality and ethics seems more explicit, one can almost see the cruel presence of the modern stage.
