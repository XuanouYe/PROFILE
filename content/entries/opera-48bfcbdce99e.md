At Hamburgische Staatsoper, Claus Guth’s 2008 production. The three most recent general music directors have all conducted this Walküre: Simone Young at the premiere, Nagano for the 2018 revival, and the new director Omer Meir Wellber for the 2026 revival. The staging clearly identifies Wotan’s role in the whole Ring story, constructing a cramped, minimal, indifferent model that reduces fate to a controlled mechanical apparatus. Wotan makes the rules. From the outset he can act from outside the model, setting Siegmund and Sieglinde’s performance in motion with a single gesture. Is The Truman Show actually the most Wagnerian thing of all? Eventually even the god can no longer understand or control the world he has created; only through repeatedly negating himself can he perish. In Act III, the imprisoned and demythologized Valkyrie, the institutional space and Wotan’s descent still retain the myth of an original power relationship. The farewell between father and daughter loses its ritual quality, yet Brünnhilde can still decide how to lie down. In an everyday gesture she puts down her pillow; Wotan arranges her blanket and shoes, then lights the fire. The scene has an intense density of human feeling, while the ignition of the flames returns it to the edge of abstraction.
