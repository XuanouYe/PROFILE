At Bayerische Staatsoper, Damiano Michieletto’s new 2024 production. This Donizetti opera, absent from German stages for many years, has returned. In Act I the military camp is gradually assembled; in Act II the mansion is progressively cleared out and dismantled. Shadows then answer the vibrations of voices within the swaying, brightly lit set. The period costumes, makeup and props feel like a joke played by this modern theatre. Michieletto turns Donizetti’s signature devices into entertainment: the lives of aristocrats and soldiers become role-playing games, forever marching on the spot. The seriousness of the work’s satire of the former and romantic praise of the latter simply disappears. This seems very different from the thoroughly explicit symbolism of Michieletto’s Aida, which premiered last year.
