At Bayerische Staatsoper, Simon Stone’s 2019 production. The setting uses contemporary domestic interiors. Marie’s room retains its order in Act I, opens into a disorder of staggered levels when the ghosts appear in Act II, then becomes orderly again in Act III. Marie’s sacred room turns into an absurdly cramped space plastered with photographs. Hallucinations appear at several points and are frightening enough. At the start of the overture, a white sheet is pulled away to reveal IKEA furnishings, with posters of Blow-Up and Pierrot le Fou on the walls as little allusions. The movement is highly sexual, including the scarf’s use as a BDSM prop and a fully nude onstage shower. Marietta’s Song is actually sung using a living-room karaoke setup. Flickering light from the room’s ambient fixtures replaces theatrical spotlights, greatly reducing the visual drama at the climaxes.
