At Bayerische Staatsoper, Richard Jones’s 2019 production. A classic children’s opera with unexpectedly conventional visual design. Many parents have brought their children, and the atmosphere is perfect for Christmas. The fable’s simplified narrative makes it accessible; opera still knows how to attract children. Humperdinck’s adaptation preserves all the qualities of the Brothers Grimm: a single narrative path, an unexpected adventure and a happy ending. His vivid use of folk melody is an enduring musical virtue. The poor family’s struggle to survive, the gingerbread house’s temptation and trap—these rather contrived symbols can still be reawakened in a modern context. The Staatsoper production cleverly gives stage and story a shared simplicity through flat, graphic settings. Restless lighting and ever-enlarged painted backdrops use skewed perspectives to probe the human desires projected by the scenery. Like the gingerbread house itself, it is bright and sugary on the outside, but filled with the coldness of industrial civilization within.
