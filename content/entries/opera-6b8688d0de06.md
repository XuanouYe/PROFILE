Suddenly, Kratzer seems to be no longer throwing stage space around, respecting the budget. Unlike his Ring in Munich, with its continually expanding spatial and visual narratives, dark grey semicircular steps constrict the illuminated area available for action. A stage that depends on spotlights is left with large, fixed areas of shadow. Its form is repetitive: repeated shadows, repeated glaring strips of light, a chorus repeatedly gathering and dispersing. Together they form an overlapping structure of watching, refusing to be watched, and compulsory watching.
