The premiere of Krzysztof Warlikowski's new 2025 production. A Russian hallucination in a downpour. The set is an enclosed space, with light appearing in different places. Káťa's isolation becomes an image, projected and played back. Like the false appearance of her love, it becomes a shadow onstage. Reality's boundaries are continually broken in the rotation. The minimal set refers to no specific time or place, yet remains effective throughout. Janáček's musical characterization is truly precious. The brass often howls at full volume, holding anxiety, longing and shattered hope within it.
