At Staatsoper Unter den Linden, Patrice Chéreau’s 2013 production. Finally, a chance to hear Simone Young conduct. The staging is rather uninspiring, though it fits the Berlinale currently taking place. A simple, constricted outdoor setting tells the complex ancient Greek revenge myth as though under confinement. The soprano’s extreme singing, continually pushed towards a climax, and the orchestra’s highly dynamic playing interact with the fear of death. Every pause, silence, stare, approach and retreat conceals a crisis about to erupt. Strauss retells this destructive tragedy through the pull between reason and desire, avoiding the barbarity of Elektra that Sophocles presents through opposing figures. Under this kind of writing, the conflict between reality and ethics seems more explicit; one can almost see the cruel presence of the modern stage.
