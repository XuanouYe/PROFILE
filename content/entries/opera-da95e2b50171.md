Strauss's early twentieth-century meta-opera. When the score in the prologue is handed to the conductor in the pit, the performance within the performance connects with the actual space of the stage. A collection of small, clever ideas, some of which carried over into Carsen's later Don Giovanni. Here the collision of the two forms is not made explicit in the stage imagery - a completely different approach from the same director's 2025 The Excursions of Mr. Brouček. As the audience enters, dancers practise in front of mirrors. Someone presses a playback button onstage, and the overture begins in the pit before the house lights have gone down. The opera proper abandons the picturesque island for an almost empty black space, surrounded by many similar bodies. The expression is minimal, with attention entrusted entirely to coloratura and dance. After Bacchus arrives, light gradually opens up the enclosed background. What began as scattered reflections in mirrors now acquires a clear direction, finally achieving a kind of transcendence.
