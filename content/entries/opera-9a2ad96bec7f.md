Abstract eighteenth-century settings: Act I feels like entering a black-and-white film, Act II surrounds Rococo painting with demons, and Act III turns into a mirrored stage. The brass lines provide steady support beneath the singing melodies, while the comedy of mistaken identities and games conceals sadness. At the end of Act III, Sophie and Octavian are actually hoisted up on wires, wanting to fly. Apart from occasional startling fortissimo entries that are not quite together, the orchestra seems to come unstuck in the soft violin tremolos at the end of every act.
